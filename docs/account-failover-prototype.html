Account switching

Orca Flow UX draft · fixture identities only · nothing here reads accounts or touches Orca

Fixture

Behaviour

  • On by default. Every eligible login of the same provider is used, in discovery order, with no setup, prompt or picker.
  • Eligible means discovered, distinct, inside the workspace’s authorized sharing scope, not excluded and not limited. System default is an alias for one login, never a second account.
  • Discovering a login doesn’t authorize it. A login outside the scope (the Acme Codex login here) is never sent Atlas’s work, even when it has capacity.
  • When Orca can’t establish the scope, the chat stays where it is and the waiting line says why.
  • Healthy work shows nothing. A successful switch leaves one muted record. When everything is limited, one waiting line shows the earliest known reset and Check now. It doesn’t cycle through accounts.

Override (only when sought)

  • Reached from the gear in the Orca Flow sidebar, or from Account switching inside a record’s details.
  • One switch turns it off. Unchecking a login excludes it. New logins in scope are used automatically.
  • On Orca 1.4.272 nothing appears in the conversation beyond today’s wait for the reset.
